## Wordle at a Glance

Wordle popularized the **daily, 5-letter** guessing format. You get **six tries**, and color feedback (🟩🟨⬛) guides each guess. It’s:

- **Simple & social**: one shared puzzle per day
- **Quick**: 2–5 minutes
- **Great warm-up** for vocabulary and pattern recognition

**Limitations:** fixed word length (5), limited replay per day, and minimal progression for players who want to grind or scale difficulty.

## Strategy That Transfers: From Wordle → WordSkull

**1) Letter Frequency Still Rules**  
Openers with common letters help in both games. In WordSkull’s shorter lengths (3–4), prioritize **high-frequency consonants** (R, S, T, N, L) and vowels (A, E).

**2) Pattern Recognition**  
Word families (e.g., `-ING`, `-ATE`, `-EST`) are useful in WordSkull’s longer modes. See our **[6-letter “ING” list](/words-list/all-6-letter-words-for-word-games)** to train.

**3) Elimination Mindset**  
Every guess should either **confirm** letters or **rule out** options. This is crucial in **Reapers** and **Royal Lichen** where word lengths expand.

**4) Short-Word Drills**  
Sharpen your speed with **3–5 letter** practice:

- **[All 3-letter words](/words-list/all-3-letter-words-for-word-games)** (great for quick fills)
- **[All 4-letter words](/words-list/all-4-letter-words-for-word-games)**
- **[All 5-letter words](/words-list/all-5-letter-words-for-word-games)**

**5) Long-Word Endurance**  
If you enjoy Wordle but crave depth, step into **7–9 letters** with **Reapers** and **Royal Lichen**.
